# 0002. Determinant-to-antimicrobial mapping v1 for the E. coli baseline

- Status: accepted (prespecified)
- Date: 2026-10-18
- Applies to: `benchmark/mappings/ecoli_amrfinder_drug_mapping_v1.tsv` and
  `.json`, generated by
  `scripts/ecoli_research_v1/build_amrfinder_drug_mapping.py`; read by
  `bin/genotype_baseline.py` 1.0.0
- Fixed before: any AMRFinderPlus result for the research cohort was read and
  before any genotype was compared with AST. The mapping files were committed
  on their own, ahead of the baseline code, so the order can be checked in the
  history.

## Context

AMRFinderPlus reports determinants and a broad class/subclass. It does not say
which of the 25 study antimicrobials (16 primary, 9 secondary, decision 0001)
a determinant affects, and its subclass strings (for example `CEPHALOSPORIN`
or `BETA-LACTAM`) cannot be turned into drug-level calls without guessing. The
baseline needs one explicit, reviewable rule per determinant and drug.

## Decision

### Sources

Drug-level phenotypes come from two curated, peer-reviewed tables built for
predicting phenotype from genotype, pinned to fixed commits:

- ResFinder gene phenotypes (`resfinder_db`, tag `resfinder-4.7.2`, commit
  `852f367c`), one row per gene variant with the antimicrobials it affects and
  PMIDs (Bortolaia et al. 2020, PMID 32780112);
- PointFinder E. coli mutation phenotypes (`pointfinder_db`, tag
  `resfinder-4.7.2`, commit `694919f5`), including the mutations that only
  count together with a gyrA codon 83 change (Zankari et al. 2017, PMID
  29091202).

The set of symbols comes from the AMRFinderPlus database 2026-08-07.1
(`fam.tsv`, `AMRProt.fa`, E. coli point mutation tables; Feldgarden et al.
2021, PMID 34135355). Hashes of every input are in the mapping JSON.

### How a rule is made

1. A determinant is an exact AMRFinderPlus element symbol. There is no class
   matching and no text matching against drug names.
2. A symbol that is a ResFinder gene name gets that gene's phenotypes. ResFinder
   letter variants of one allele (blaTEM-1A to -1D) count as that allele.
3. A symbol missing from ResFinder (a family node such as `blaCTX-M`, or a newer
   allele) uses the ResFinder genes under its AMRFinderPlus node: a drug is
   `predicts_resistance` if every such gene lists it, `uncertain` if only some do.
4. A PointFinder mutation maps to the AMRFinderPlus symbol with the same gene,
   position and amino acid (or base). Drugs listed for the mutation alone are
   `predicts_resistance`; drugs listed only with a required gyrA change carry
   that change in `requires` and are uncertain without it.
5. Source phenotype terms are translated to PR #5 drug names by an explicit table
   (for example `Amoxicillin+Clavulanic acid` to `amoxicillin-clavulanic acid`).
   "Unknown <class>" entries give `uncertain` for the study drugs of that class.
6. Five study drugs have no source term of their own and are inferred, each row
   flagged `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`:
   - levofloxacin from ciprofloxacin or fluoroquinolone (same targets; Hooper &
     Jacoby 2015, PMID 26190223);
   - sulfisoxazole from sulfamethoxazole (same target, dihydropteroate synthase);
   - ceftriaxone from cefotaxime when ceftriaxone itself is not listed;
   - ceftiofur from cefotaxime or ceftriaxone;
   - cefazolin from cephalothin, or from an oxyimino-cephalosporin, since an
     enzyme that hydrolyses those also hydrolyses narrow-spectrum cephalosporins
     (Bush & Jacoby 2010, PMID 19995920).
7. Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole is positive only with both a trimethoprim
   determinant and a sulfonamide determinant; one alone is uncertain. Clinical
   resistance to the combination generally needs both an alternative DHFR and
   an alternative DHPS (Tagg et al. 2019, PMID 30202900).
8. `blaEC`, the chromosomal AmpC that every E. coli carries, is
   `does_not_predict` for all beta-lactams. Raised AmpC expression is captured
   by the PointFinder promoter mutations (Jacoby 2009, PMID 19136439).
   `ampC_G-15GG` has no exact PointFinder counterpart and is `uncertain`.

### Evidence and status rules in the baseline

- Only `element_type == AMR` counts. STRESS and VIRULENCE elements and
  AMRFinderPlus `--plus` output are not used.
- Hits by `INTERNAL_STOP`, `PARTIALP` or `PARTIALX` make a rule uncertain
  rather than positive.
- An isolate is determinant-positive for a drug if any `predicts_resistance`
  rule applies with its `requires` met, or if every component of a combination
  rule is present. Otherwise it is uncertain if any uncertain rule applies or a
  `requires` is unmet. Otherwise it is determinant-negative.
- Determinant-negative means no determinant satisfying this mapping was found
  in a successfully analysed genome. It is not biological susceptibility.

### Rule variants (prespecified)

| Variant | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `primary` | uncertain endpoints left out of the metrics |
| `uncertain_as_positive` | sensitivity analysis |
| `uncertain_as_negative` | sensitivity analysis |
| `fq_two_mechanisms` | ciprofloxacin and levofloxacin positive only with at least two predicting determinants |

The last variant addresses a known mismatch. ResFinder and PointFinder phenotypes
describe departure from the wild type (ECOFF), while PR #5 phenotypes are
clinical categories. A single QRDR mutation or a single PMQR gene often leaves
the ciprofloxacin MIC below the clinical breakpoint (Hooper & Jacoby 2015;
Strahilevitz et al. 2009, PMID 19822894). The primary analysis follows the
sources. All single-mechanism fluoroquinolone rules are flagged for review.

## Result

46,701 rules over 25 antimicrobials. Of the 7,884 reportable AMR symbols,
2,372 have exact ResFinder entries and 3,363 a family consensus; 2,149 have no
source evidence and no rule. Four PointFinder E. coli entries have no
AMRFinderPlus symbol: gyrA D87A, parC F60K, and the -13/-15/-16 promoter
insertions. 6,209 rules are `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`: inferred drugs,
single-mechanism fluoroquinolone rules, plus the four `ampC_G-15GG` rows.

## Consequences

- Detection (AMRFinderPlus) and interpretation (this mapping) are separate
  steps, and either can be changed and rerun without the other.
- The mapping is not tuned to the data. `mapping_audit.tsv` flags rules whose
  carriers are mostly susceptible, or non-predicting rules whose carriers are
  mostly resistant, for review only. Any change goes into a new mapping version
  (`..._v2`) with its own decision record, and is reported as exploratory.
- The rule set is defined independently of any machine-learning performance.

## Alternatives considered

- AMRFinderPlus class/subclass as the drug call: rejected because it is class
  matching, and subclass strings such as `BETA-LACTAM` do not name drugs.
- CARD's antibiotic ontology: not pinned or checked here. The ResFinder tables
  are drug-level, organism-oriented and come with PMIDs.
- Clinical-breakpoint rules for fluoroquinolones as the primary analysis:
  kept as the prespecified `fq_two_mechanisms` variant instead of replacing the
  source mapping.
